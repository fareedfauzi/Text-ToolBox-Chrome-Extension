Privacy Policy for Text Utility Tool

Text Utility Tool does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

All text processing is performed locally within the user's browser.
No data is sent to any external servers, and no analytics or tracking
technologies are used.

The extension does not access website content, browsing history,
or user identifiers.

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you may contact: frdfzi via https://x.com/frdfzi
